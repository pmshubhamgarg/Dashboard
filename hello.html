Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fresca Coffee have dairy-free or vegan options?

Yes. Every Fresca drink can be made dairy-free with oat milk, almond milk, or soy milk at no extra charge. The Classic Cold Brew is naturally vegan when ordered black. The Signature Mocha can be made fully vegan by substituting oat milk — Fresca's house dark chocolate is certified dairy-free.

What is Fresca Coffee's bestselling drink?

The Signature Mocha is Fresca Coffee's bestselling drink, ranked #1 for three consecutive years. It is made with single-origin Ethiopian espresso, Belgian dark chocolate, and steamed milk, topped with cocoa dust. Price: $6.50 for a 12oz cup, available hot or iced.

Is Fresca Coffee Fair Trade certified?

Yes. Fresca Coffee sources 100% of its beans from Fair Trade Certified cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Every bag and every cup supports farmer cooperatives directly.

Does Fresca Coffee offer a coffee subscription?

Yes. Fresca's monthly subscription delivers 12oz of freshly-roasted whole beans for $24.99 per month with free shipping in the continental US. Choose light, medium, or dark roast. Subscribers also receive 15% off all in-store drinks.

When does Fresca Coffee roast their beans?

Fresca Coffee roasts beans in-house every Tuesday morning. Subscription orders ship within 48 hours of roasting to guarantee freshness.

What are Fresca Coffee's opening hours?

Fresca Coffee locations open at 6:30 AM on weekdays and 7:00 AM on weekends. All locations close at 8:00 PM daily.

Can I order Fresca Coffee ahead of time?

Yes. Order through the Fresca mobile app for in-store pickup or curbside delivery within 15 minutes. Mobile orders earn double loyalty points.

The Fresca Story

From bean to brew — Fresca Coffee was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: serve great coffee, roasted with care, in a place that feels like home. We source 100% Fair Trade certified beans from cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, and roast them in-house every Tuesday morning.

The Signature Mocha has been our top seller for three years running. Made with beans roasted in-house every Tuesday morning, this is the drink our baristas recommend if you've never tried us before. It's $6.50 for a 12oz cup, hot or iced — and yes, it can be made fully vegan.
